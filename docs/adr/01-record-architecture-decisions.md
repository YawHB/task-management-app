# Record Architecture Decisions


> **Status:** 🟢 Accepted  
> **Date:** 2026-07-23  
> **ID:** #01

---

## Context
This project serves as a portfolio piece demonstrating proficiency with industry-standard technologies.
Therefore, we believe documenting all significant architectural decisions will be valuable for developing a technical log for reference in the future. It will also benefit whoever is interested in the evolution of the project and the considerations that influenced the software.



## Decision
We will keep a collection of architectural decision records (ADR) as a tool to document all significant decisions influencing the software architecture.  
We will use the ADR template, created by Michael Nygard, a widely adopted standard in the industry, which contains the following sections:
- Title - The title of the ADR
- Status - Whether it is proposed, accepted or superseded.
- Context - Describes the problem and explains why a decision is needed.
- Decision - The actual decision
- Consequences - How will this affect the team, development etc.


## Consequences

### Positive
- Promotes team alignment toward a consistent direction in software development.
- Encourages the team to be conscious about functional and non-functional requirements before implementation.
- Trains the team's ability to consider multiple solutions to a problem, weighing benefits and trade-offs.

### Trade-offs
- Adds another analytical activity that draws from development time. This is especially a trade-off for our two-person team.
- Risk of implementing suboptimal decisions if unaware of better alternatives.
- Best solution may involve unfamiliar technologies, requiring a choice between learning on the fly or selecting a less appropriate but easier-to-implement alternative
